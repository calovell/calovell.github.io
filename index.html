About

I’m an operator first.

My work sits where strategy meets execution: designing growth systems, improving sales and service operations, and using data and technology to make insurance distribution work better.

Today, I serve as President of Insurance at Centerfield, leading strategy, innovation, growth, and profitability across the insurance business.

My background spans insurance, insurtech, performance marketing, strategic partnerships, and entrepreneurship. I’ve led operating teams at scale, built new businesses, and worked across both incumbent and technology-driven models.

I’m particularly interested in practical AI: where it changes economics, improves decision quality, or lets people spend more time on work that requires judgment and trust.
